KOKO Networks · software and hardware QA

testing connected systems in the field and on the bench.

When a device lost power or a network connection dropped, did the app still show the right state? At KOKO Networks, I tested those handoffs across firmware, cloud services, and applications on real hardware.

Gateways and cloud integration

On the next-generation build, my integration work covered embedded Linux gateways, AWS IoT Greengrass, and AWS IoT Core. On Raspberry Pi, I checked boot behavior, peripherals, and service stability over long runs. In IoT Core, I tested device provisioning, MQTT permissions, and whether device shadows matched the hardware state after a disconnect.

Android and desktop applications

On Android, I tested Bluetooth Low Energy onboarding, notifications during hardware operations, and MQTT recovery when the app moved between foreground and background. I also tested desktop apps. When a failure crossed layers, I worked with embedded, firmware, backend, and mobile engineers to isolate it.

Hardware testing and automation

I wrote Python simulators to generate device telemetry and stress-test data ingestion. On the hardware bench, I integrated system software into tests and used measurements from batteries, pumps, and valves to inform component selection.

One result was the automated pump rig. I led development of its Python test system, which cut the pump test cycle from 12 months to 2. We moved test data from CSV files into InfluxDB and monitored runs in Grafana.
